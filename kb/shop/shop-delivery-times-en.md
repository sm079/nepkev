Delivery usually takes:

- Kathmandu valley: 1 to 3 working days.
- Other cities: 3 to 7 working days.
- Remote areas: up to 10 working days.

Delivery inside the valley costs NPR 100 and is free for orders above NPR 2,000. Outside the valley the charge is shown at checkout. You can track the parcel on the order page; we also send an SMS when it is out for delivery.
