updated_at: 2026-08-05

Electronics carry the manufacturer's warranty shown on the product page, usually 1 year. Warranty claims go to the brand's service centre; download the invoice from the order page and take it with you.

If a device is dead on arrival or develops a defect within 7 days of delivery, we replace it directly; report it as a damaged or defective item. Physical damage and liquid damage are not covered by the warranty.
