title: Mobile banking login problems
updated_at: 2026-09-09
---

If you forgot your mobile banking password, reset it in the app with your account number and an OTP sent to your registered mobile number.

After 3 wrong password attempts, mobile banking is blocked for 24 hours. When you change phones you must register the new device again with an OTP. If your registered mobile number has changed, update it at a branch first.
