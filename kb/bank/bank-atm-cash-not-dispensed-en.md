title: ATM debited the account but gave no cash
updated_at: 2026-09-27
---

If an ATM debits your account but does not give cash, the amount is reversed automatically: within 2 working days at our own ATMs, and within 7 working days at another bank's ATM.

If the money is not back by then, file a dispute in mobile banking or at a branch with the date, time and location of the ATM. Disputes are resolved within 15 working days.
