title: Mobile banking transfer limits
updated_at: 2026-09-14
---

Through mobile banking you can transfer up to NPR 500,000 per day to accounts in this bank, and up to NPR 200,000 per day to other banks. Transfers between your own accounts have no daily limit.

Transfers to other banks made after 5 pm, or on a public holiday, settle on the next working day. To raise your limit for a one-off payment, visit a branch.
