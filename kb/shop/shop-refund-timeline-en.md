updated_at: 2026-09-02

Once a refund is approved, the money goes back to the payment method used for the order.

- Wallet payments: within 3 working days.
- Debit or credit card: 7 to 10 working days, depending on the card's bank.
- Cash on delivery: we refund to your wallet or bank account within 5 working days after you share the account details in the app.

A refund is approved when the returned item passes inspection, or at once for a cancelled prepaid order. If the money has not arrived after these periods, contact support with your order number; the case is escalated to the payments team within 24 hours.
