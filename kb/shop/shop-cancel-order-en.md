title: Cancelling an order
updated_at: 2026-08-11
---

You can cancel an order from My Orders until it is shipped, that is while its status is Placed, Confirmed or Packed. There is no cancellation fee.

Once the order is shipped it can no longer be cancelled. You can refuse the parcel at delivery, or accept it and request a return.

For prepaid orders the refund follows the normal refund timeline. Cash on delivery orders need no refund.
