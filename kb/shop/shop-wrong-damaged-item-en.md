title: Wrong, damaged or defective items
updated_at: 2026-08-27
---

If you received the wrong product, size or colour, or the item arrived damaged or defective, report it within 48 hours of delivery. Attach photos of the item and the package and give your order number.

We replace the item at no cost, or refund it if a replacement is not in stock. Pickup of the wrong or damaged item is free; do not throw it away until the case is closed.
