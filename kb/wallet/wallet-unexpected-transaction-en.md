title: A transaction you do not recognise
updated_at: 2026-10-01
---

If you see a transaction you did not make, change your PIN at once and block the wallet from Profile > Block wallet, or call the 24-hour helpline.

Report the transaction within 7 days so that we can investigate; investigations take up to 10 working days. We never ask for your PIN, OTP or password. Anyone who does is not from the wallet.
