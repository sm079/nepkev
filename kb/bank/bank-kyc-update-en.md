title: Updating your account KYC
updated_at: 2026-08-22
---

The bank asks you to update your KYC when an identity document expires or your details change. Until it is updated, withdrawals and transfers from the account may be restricted; deposits still work.

Update KYC at any branch, or upload the documents in mobile banking under Profile > Update KYC. Updates are processed within 3 working days.
