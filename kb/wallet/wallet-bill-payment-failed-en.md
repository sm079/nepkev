title: Electricity, water or internet bill payment failed
updated_at: 2026-09-12
---

If a bill payment fails but your wallet is debited, the amount is reversed within 3 working days.

Sometimes the payment reaches the utility even though the wallet shows Failed. The utility can take up to 24 hours to update its records, so check your bill status after a day before paying again. Keep the receipt from the transaction page in case the utility asks for it.
