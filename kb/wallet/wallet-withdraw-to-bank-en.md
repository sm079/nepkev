title: Sending wallet money to a bank account
updated_at: 2026-09-03
---

Verified wallets can send up to NPR 100,000 per day to a bank account. The money usually arrives within minutes; on public holidays and weekends it can take until the next working day.

The first 3 transfers each month are free, after that each costs NPR 10. If the bank has not credited the money by the end of the next working day, report it from the transaction page.
