title: Checkout payment failed but money was deducted
updated_at: 2026-09-05
---

If a payment fails at checkout but the money is deducted, no order is created. The amount is reversed automatically to the account it came from: within 24 hours for wallet payments, and within 5 working days for cards and bank transfers.

You do not need to contact us during that time. Place the order again only if you still want the item. If the money is not back after 5 working days, send us the transaction ID from your bank or wallet statement.
