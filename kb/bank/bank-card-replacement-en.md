title: Getting a replacement card
updated_at: 2026-08-14
---

You can request a replacement debit card in mobile banking or at any branch. The fee is NPR 500, charged to your account.

The new card is ready for collection at the branch you choose within 5 working days; bring an identity document. Set the PIN for the new card in mobile banking under Cards > Set PIN.
