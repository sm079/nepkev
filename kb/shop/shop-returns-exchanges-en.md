You can return an item within 7 days of delivery if it is unused, with tags and original packaging. An exchange for a different size or colour is allowed once per order, subject to stock.

These items cannot be returned: innerwear, cosmetics once opened, perishable groceries and digital vouchers.

Start a return from My Orders. Inside Kathmandu valley we arrange a pickup within 2 working days. Outside the valley, send the item by courier; we refund the shipping cost up to NPR 200. If the item you received is wrong or damaged, see the wrong or damaged item policy instead.
