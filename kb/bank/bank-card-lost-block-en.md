title: Blocking a lost or stolen card
updated_at: 2026-09-20
---

If your debit card is lost or stolen, block it at once in mobile banking under Cards > Block card, or call the 24-hour helpline. Blocking takes effect immediately.

A card blocked as lost or stolen cannot be unblocked; request a replacement card instead. If you only misplaced the card, use Cards > Freeze card, which you can undo yourself.
