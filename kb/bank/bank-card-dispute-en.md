title: Disputing a card transaction you did not make
updated_at: 2026-09-29
---

If your card statement shows a transaction you did not make, block the card first, then report the transaction within 7 days in mobile banking or at a branch.

Disputes for online or other card-not-present payments can take up to 45 days, because the merchant's bank must respond. No temporary credit is given while the dispute is open. You will get an SMS when it is decided.
