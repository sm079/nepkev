title: Transfer to another bank shows success but was not credited
updated_at: 2026-10-03
---

When a transfer to another bank shows Successful but the beneficiary has not received it, the receiving bank normally credits it within 1 working day.

If the beneficiary's bank cannot credit it, for example because the account number or name does not match, the money returns to your account within 3 working days. Share the transfer's reference number with the beneficiary so that their bank can trace it.
