title: Money sent but the recipient did not receive it
updated_at: 2026-09-25
---

Wallet-to-wallet transfers are normally instant. If the recipient has not received the money within 30 minutes, first check the recipient's number in your transaction history.

If the number is correct, report the transfer from its transaction page. We trace it within 2 working days. A transfer stuck in Pending for 72 hours is reversed to your wallet automatically.
