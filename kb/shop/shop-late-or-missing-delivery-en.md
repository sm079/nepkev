title: Late, lost or not-received parcels
updated_at: 2026-09-18
---

If your order has not arrived 2 days after the latest promised date, report it from the order page. We open an investigation with the courier and reply within 48 hours.

If tracking says delivered but you did not receive the parcel, report it within 48 hours of the delivered status. We check the courier's proof of delivery. If the parcel cannot be found, we send the order again or give a full refund, as you choose.
