title: Money sent to the wrong number
updated_at: 2026-08-29
---

We cannot reverse a completed transfer without the recipient's consent. If you sent money to the wrong number by mistake, report it at once from the transaction page.

We contact the recipient and ask them to return the money; most cases are settled within 5 working days. If the number is not registered with the wallet, the money is held for 7 days and then returns to you automatically. Always check the name shown before you confirm a transfer.
