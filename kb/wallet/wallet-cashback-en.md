Cashback from an offer is credited to your wallet within 7 days of the qualifying transaction. The offer page lists which payments qualify and the most you can earn.

Cashback is not given for transactions that are later refunded or reversed, and it is taken back if a qualifying payment is refunded after it was credited. Cashback balance cannot be sent to a bank account.
