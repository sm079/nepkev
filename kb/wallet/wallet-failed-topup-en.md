title: Mobile top-up failed but money was deducted
updated_at: 2026-09-22
---

If a mobile recharge or data pack top-up fails but the money leaves your wallet, it is reversed to your wallet automatically within 24 hours.

If the top-up shows Pending, wait up to 2 hours before trying again, so that you are not charged twice. If the money has not come back after 24 hours, raise a ticket from the transaction's page; it already carries the transaction ID.
