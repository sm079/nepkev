title: Loading money from a bank account
updated_at: 2026-08-18
---

You can load money into the wallet from a linked bank account through mobile banking, internet banking or a debit card.

If your bank account is debited but the wallet balance does not increase, the load is usually settled automatically within 2 hours. If it is not, the bank reverses the amount to your account within 3 to 5 working days. Keep the bank's reference number; we need it if you raise a ticket.
