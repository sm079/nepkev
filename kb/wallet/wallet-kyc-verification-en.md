updated_at: 2026-09-08

To verify your wallet (KYC), upload a citizenship certificate, passport or national ID card, a recent photo and your current address. Verification is reviewed within 2 working days.

Common reasons for rejection are a blurry or cropped document photo, a name that does not match the wallet account, and an expired document. You can resubmit at once after a rejection. Until KYC is approved, the wallet stays at the lower unverified limits.
