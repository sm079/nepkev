An OTP is valid for 5 minutes. If it does not arrive, check that your phone has network and free SMS space, then request a new one after 60 seconds. At most 5 OTPs can be requested per hour.

If you forgot your PIN, choose Forgot PIN on the login screen and reset it with an OTP and your date of birth. After 5 wrong PIN attempts the account is locked for 30 minutes. We never ask for your PIN or OTP by phone or message.
