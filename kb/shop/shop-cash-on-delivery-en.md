updated_at: 2026-07-30

Cash on delivery is available for orders up to NPR 25,000, with no extra fee. You can pay the rider in cash or by scanning the rider's QR code.

It is not available for gift cards, for some sellers marked prepaid only, or for orders above NPR 25,000. Please keep the exact amount ready; riders may not carry change.
