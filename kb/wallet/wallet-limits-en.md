Limits depend on whether your wallet is verified (KYC approved):

- Unverified: payments up to NPR 5,000 per day; sending money to other people is not allowed.
- Verified: up to NPR 100,000 per day and NPR 500,000 per month, with at most NPR 50,000 in a single transaction.

Daily limits reset at midnight Nepal time. You can see how much of today's limit is left under Profile > Limits.
